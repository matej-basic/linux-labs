OBJECTIVE
  Open a service and a custom port in the public zone of the running
  firewalld.

PREREQUISITES
  - Rocky Linux 8 or 9 with firewalld installed. The lab starts the
    firewalld service if it is stopped.
  - Root access through sudo.

TASKS
  1. Make sure the firewalld service is running.
  2. Allow the http service in the public zone.
  3. Allow port 8080/tcp in the public zone.
  4. Both rules must be active in the running firewall. Making them
     permanent is not required and is not graded.

EXPECTED RESULT
  The running firewall lists http among the services and 8080/tcp
  among the ports of the public zone. Existing rules, including
  ssh, stay as they are.

NOTES
  - Do not remove or change the ssh service, or your session may be
    cut off.
  - Reference: man firewall-cmd.

GRADING
  labctl grade firewall-01
