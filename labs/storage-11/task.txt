OBJECTIVE
  An application on {{HOSTNAME}} reports that its file system is
  almost full, and a second file system no longer mounts. Find the
  causes and repair both without losing data.

PREREQUISITES
  - Root access through sudo. Rocky Linux 8 and 9 behave the same.
  - /etc/fstab mounts two image files by their paths:

      /srv/storage-11-appdata.img   on /srv/appdata
      /srv/storage-11-archive.img   on /srv/archive

  - The service app-logger.service writes the application log on
    /srv/appdata.

TASKS
  1. /srv/appdata stays mounted from /srv/storage-11-appdata.img and
     is less than 50% used, as df reports it.
  2. The files in /srv/appdata/data are unchanged. Everything else on
     /srv/appdata is disposable, and the disposable files that take up
     its space are deleted.
  3. app-logger.service is active, and no process keeps a deleted file
     on /srv/appdata open.
  4. /etc/fstab passes the fstab verification of findmnt without
     errors.
  5. /srv/archive is mounted from /srv/storage-11-archive.img through
     its entry in /etc/fstab. The entry still names the image by its
     path and keeps the option nofail. The files on /srv/archive are
     intact.
  6. All other lines of /etc/fstab are unchanged.

EXPECTED RESULT
  df and du agree on /srv/appdata, the application keeps logging, and
  both file systems mount from /etc/fstab.

PRACTICE (not graded)
  - Compare the use of /srv/appdata as df shows it with the size of
    its files as du shows it, before and after the repair.
  - Unmount /srv/archive and mount it again by its mount point only.

NOTES
  - Free the space and repair the configuration. Do not enlarge or
    recreate a file system.
  - The tool lsof is not installed. Everything needed can be found
    under /proc.
  - Do not reboot {{HOSTNAME}} during the lab. Repair the running
    system.
  - labctl reset stops and removes app-logger.service, unmounts both
    file systems, removes the images and the mount points and puts
    /etc/fstab back as it was before the lab.
  - Reference: man df, man du, man proc (the fd directory),
    man findmnt, man fstab, man mount, man blkid, man systemctl.

GRADING
  labctl grade storage-11
