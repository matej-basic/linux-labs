OBJECTIVE

  now.

PREREQUISITES
  Root access is required to manage services. Use sudo as
  {{LAB_USER}}. The lab works the same on Rocky Linux 8 and 9.

TASKS
  1. The unit test-service.service already exists on this system.
     Configure it to start automatically at boot.
  2. Make test-service.service active (running) now.

PRACTICE (not graded)

  unit files. Find out whether the service is enabled and whether
  it is running, using two separate queries.

NOTES

    evaluated.
